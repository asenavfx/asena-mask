Asena Mask 1.0.5: security update. Updates are now digitally signed, so only genuine Asena Mask updates can install. Anonymous stats and the Discord status are now off unless you turn them on (Settings > Privacy). File names and update notes always show as plain text.
Asena Mask 1.0.4: the live highlight now follows a picked subject between your picked frames (points and boxes glide from one picked frame to the next, shown as dashed markers, so scrubbing shows where the subject probably is), skips duplicate (held) frames so slideshow-style clips process much faster, new Edge cleanup setting that removes colour fringes around the cutout, blurred-original and gradient backgrounds, new Fast preview and Best quality presets, Discord now shows "Playing Asena Mask" like any game (hide it in Discord > Settings > Activity Privacy), a cleaner and smoother live highlight (stray specks and pinholes removed, smooth outline, same clean-up used when the render starts), a much quicker first live preview (the AI now loads when you open a clip, warms up, and comes back after a render), the "done in" time now counts from the moment you press Run, and a notice listing the exact times where the cutout may have glitched so you can fix them in Trim.
Asena Mask 1.0.2: new Stability setting that smooths flicker, holes and torn edges between frames (on by default), the Trim window now opens on the frame you are looking at, a fix for the Retro look showing all three setting tabs piled up at first launch, a clear message with the fix for older graphics cards (GTX 10-series) that could not run the AI, and anonymous usage stats (totals only, turn off in Settings > Privacy).
